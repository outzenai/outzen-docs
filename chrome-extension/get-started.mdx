## Install and connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Outzen to Chrome">
    [Install Outzen from the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/elhogjlbminnodlabeimklikggfaipii) and, if you like, pin it to your toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open LinkedIn">
    Go to any LinkedIn page. A small Outzen icon appears on the right edge of the window. If a LinkedIn tab was already open, reload it once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Outzen icon">
    The drawer opens on the right. The toolbar icon opens it too. Close it with the **×** in the drawer or the toolbar icon. You can drag the icon up or down to move it out of the way.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your account">
    Click **Connect to Outzen** and sign in. You only do this once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the extension adds to LinkedIn

- **The drawer.** Opens beside the page and shows what Outzen found on it.
- **Checkboxes and a Outzen bar** on people searches and on your own connections, followers and following. See [Searches and imports](/chrome-extension/searches-and-imports).
- **A Outzen button on profiles**, next to LinkedIn's own buttons. See [On a LinkedIn profile](/chrome-extension/profiles).

All of it comes from Outzen and is removed when you uninstall the extension.

## Around the drawer

- **Home.** The house button at the top opens Home on any page: a welcome, shortcuts to **Lists**, **Campaigns**, **Inbox** and **Settings** in Outzen, and one-click ways to start an import. If you are on a page Outzen can read, **Back to this page** takes you back.
- **Workspace.** If you belong to more than one workspace, switch between them from the name at the top.
- **Credits.** Your credit balance sits next to the workspace name. It updates after anything that spends credits.
- **The ⋯ menu.** **Open Outzen** takes you to your workspace, **How to use** opens a short illustrated guide, and **Sign out** disconnects the extension.

## Which pages it works on

| On this LinkedIn page | What you get |
|---|---|
| A profile | A Outzen button on the page, and the drawer shows where the person stands in Outzen. [Add them](/chrome-extension/profiles). |
| A people search | Checkboxes and a Outzen bar on the results. [Pick people or bulk import](/chrome-extension/searches-and-imports). |
| A Sales Navigator people search | The same checkboxes and bar, and bulk import up to 2,500 people. See [Sales Navigator](/chrome-extension/searches-and-imports#sales-navigator). |
| Your connections, followers or following | The same checkboxes and bar, and a bulk import that knows how many people the list has. See [Searches and imports](/chrome-extension/searches-and-imports#your-connections-followers-and-following). |
| A company page | **Open employees**. See [other pages](/chrome-extension/other-pages). |
| An event page | **Open attendees**. See [other pages](/chrome-extension/other-pages). |
| Anything else (your feed, messages) | The drawer keeps the last page it could work with, marked **You've left that page**, and your picks are kept. Other pages aren't read. |
